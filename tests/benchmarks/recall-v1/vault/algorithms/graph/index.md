# graph

## Concepts

* [A* Search](/algorithms/graph/a-star-search.md) - Guiding shortest-path search with an admissible heuristic to expand fewer nodes.
* [BFS and DFS](/algorithms/graph/bfs-dfs.md) - Breadth-first and depth-first traversal and the problems each naturally solves.
* [Dijkstra Shortest Path](/algorithms/graph/dijkstra.md) - Greedy single-source shortest paths over non-negative edge weights with a priority queue.
* [Minimum Spanning Tree](/algorithms/graph/minimum-spanning-tree.md) - Connecting all nodes at least total cost with Kruskal or Prim.
